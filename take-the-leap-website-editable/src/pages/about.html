About Me

Victoria is an author, speaker and Pitch and Communication Consultant dedicated to helping founders and business owners take bold action in life and business.

With a passion for personal growth and clear, confident communication, she founded Take the Leap to inspire people to move past fear and step into opportunity. Today, that philosophy underpins her work helping founders refine their pitches, strengthen their messaging and communicate with clarity under pressure.

Before this chapter, Victoria spent ten years as a police officer, including several years as a Detective. That experience shaped the way she communicates today — how people respond under pressure, how important clarity and trust can be, and how the way you deliver a message can completely change the outcome.

After making a life-changing move and building a startup from the ground up, Victoria learned firsthand what it means to step outside your comfort zone. She has secured significant government funding and understands both the mindset required to step forward and the structure required to succeed.

Whether through her book, masterclasses or consulting work, she is committed to helping people move past doubt, articulate their value and confidently go after what they truly want.
