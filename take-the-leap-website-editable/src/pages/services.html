Victoria Howe — Speaker | Pitch & Communication Strategist

Victoria delivers practical, high-impact sessions that help founders and business leaders communicate with clarity, confidence and conviction. Her talks focus on:

  • Refining and structuring powerful pitches
  • Communicating under pressure
  • Turning complex ideas into compelling narratives
  • Building confidence in high-stakes conversations
  • Moving from fear to decisive action

Drawing from her experience as a former police officer and entrepreneur who has built and secured funding for her own venture, Victoria understands what it means to perform when it matters. Her sessions blend mindset with structure, ensuring audiences leave not just inspired, but equipped with practical tools they can apply immediately.

Victoria's style is direct, engaging and grounded in real-world experience. Whether speaking to startup founders, accelerator cohorts or leadership teams, she helps individuals articulate their value and step forward with clarity.
